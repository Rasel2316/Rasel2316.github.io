About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at IISER Bhopal, under the supervision of Dr. Tanmay Basu. My research develops unsupervised and weakly-supervised learning methods for brain MRI segmentation, aiming to enable automated clinical analysis without expensive manual annotations.

My broader interests span probabilistic modelling, topological data analysis, and uncertainty quantification in deep learning, applied to medical imaging. I serve as a reviewer for the ML4H Symposium 2025.
