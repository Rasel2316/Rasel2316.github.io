01 · About

Decoding the Brain
Through Data

I'm a PhD scholar in Data Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal, working under the supervision of Dr. Tanmay Basu. My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and medical image analysis.

My core focus is building unsupervised and semi-supervised methods for brain MRI segmentation — developing models that can identify lesions, anomalies, and structures without relying on expensive manual annotations, making intelligent diagnostics more accessible in clinical settings.

I am also a reviewer for the ML4H Symposium 2025 and have been selected for the Doctoral Consortium at IEEE ICHI 2026.

02 · Research

Research Themes

⬡

Unsupervised Brain MRI Segmentation

Developing clustering and probabilistic methods for segmenting brain lesions and tissues in MRI scans without manual labels — using Fuzzy C-Means, MRF, and normalizing flows.

ClusteringMRFFuzzy Logic
◈

Uncertainty-Aware Deep Learning

Integrating Bayesian inference, Monte Carlo dropout, and heteroscedastic uncertainty into segmentation pipelines for interpretable, clinically trustworthy predictions.

Bayesian DLMC DropoutUNet
◎

Topology-Aware Learning

Leveraging persistent homology and topological priors to preserve structural connectivity in segmentation outputs, particularly for complex anatomical boundaries.

TDAHomologyBoundary Opt.
⬢

Histopathology Image Analysis

Building topology-aware, uncertainty-quantifying deep networks for gland and tissue segmentation in histological slides, supporting automated pathology workflows.

HistopathologyUNet++Segmentation
